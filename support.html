Here to help.

Log a drink

In Oasis, choose your drink amount with the slider, then tap the drink button after drinking the water.

Change your goal

Open Profile and use the goal controls to choose your personal daily target.

Set up reminders

In Profile, choose a reminder interval and sleep and wake times. Tap the scheduling option and allow notifications when asked. If reminders are missing, check your device notification settings.

Explore your history

Open History and select 7 or 30 days to review your logged drinks and daily average.

Correct a log

Use the delete control beside a drink entry in Oasis if you recorded it by mistake.

Report an issue

When reporting a problem, include your device model, system version, app version and the steps that caused it. Avoid sharing personal health information.
